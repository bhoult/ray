# Fractals

Every kind of fractal ray draws: what it is, its formula and distance estimate, where it came from, and its parameters. Each is a shape type: `{"type": "mandalay", "scale": 0.6}` in a scene, or Add > Fractals in the editor (the families are submenus there). `saves/fractal-catalog.json` shows them all, a family to a row; the screenshots below are from `ray SCENE --out` renders.

All of them take the common fields (`position`, `rotation`, `scale`, `material`...). A fractal colored by the `orbit` material pattern shows its orbit trap: where its iteration came closest to its center, or which level of it the surface belongs to.

Most of the catalog's formulas come from the 3D fractal community of Fractal Forums (2009 onward), many by way of [Mandelbulber 2](https://github.com/buddhi1980/mandelbulber2) (GPL-3, as ray is), whose authors collected and coded them. Each entry credits its formula's authors. Where ray's version differs (a different distance estimate, defaults or orientation), the entry says so.

The fractals by family; each links to its own page.

## Algebras

The Mandelbrot iteration in other number systems (quaternions, bicomplex numbers, hypercomplex numbers) and other polynomial and analytic maps in 3D.

| | Fractal |
| --- | --- |
| [<img src="images/fractals/quaternion.jpg" width="800" alt="Quaternion Mandelbrot">](fractals/quaternion.md) | **[Quaternion Mandelbrot](fractals/quaternion.md)** (`quaternion`)<br>The quaternion Mandelbrot set (Gomatam et al. and Bedding and Briggs, 1995): z² + c with quaternions, sliced in 3D. Squaring a quaternion keeps it in the plane of the real axis and its own imaginary part, so the set is the Mandelbrot set spun about its real axis: a lathe-turned ornament of discs and rings with the antenna for a spire. |
| [<img src="images/fractals/tetrabrot.jpg" width="800" alt="Tetrabrot">](fractals/tetrabrot.md) | **[Tetrabrot](fractals/tetrabrot.md)** (`tetrabrot`)<br>Dominic Rochon's Tetrabrot (2000): the bicomplex Mandelbrot set's slice through 1, i1 and i2. A bicomplex number is two complex numbers squared independently, so the shape is two Mandelbrot sets drawn out along the diagonals and intersected: a faceted, cubist Mandelbrot with diamond cross-sections and box-like bulbs. |
| [<img src="images/fractals/hypercomplex.jpg" width="800" alt="Hypercomplex Mandelbrot">](fractals/hypercomplex.md) | **[Hypercomplex Mandelbrot](fractals/hypercomplex.md)** (`hypercomplex`)<br>Clyde Davenport's hypercomplex Mandelbrot set: z² + c with commutative quaternions (i² = j² = -1, k² = +1), sliced through 1, i and k. |
| [<img src="images/fractals/aexion.jpg" width="800" alt="Aexion">](fractals/aexion.md) | **[Aexion](fractals/aexion.md)** (`aexion`)<br>Aexion's quadray Mandelbrot set: the point in four tetrahedral (quadray) coordinates, folded by abs and shifted, squared in four dimensions as two complex squares that trade imaginary parts. |
| [<img src="images/fractals/bristorbrot.jpg" width="800" alt="Bristorbrot">](fractals/bristorbrot.md) | **[Bristorbrot](fractals/bristorbrot.md)** (`bristorbrot`)<br>Doug Bristor's 3D Mandelbrot set, squared in his 'rotational' algebra of two imaginary units whose products depend on their order, so the two imaginary axes mix: the Mandelbrot set's outline along x, swept into a swirled, lopsided solid with a long antenna. |
| [<img src="images/fractals/buffalo.jpg" width="800" alt="Buffalo">](fractals/buffalo.md) | **[Buffalo](fractals/buffalo.md)** (`buffalo`)<br>The 3D Buffalo: the power 2 Mandelbulb's squaring with some of its components made positive (abs) before or after, a shaggy 3D cousin of the 2D Buffalo and Burning Ship (the default holds the 2D Buffalo in its xy plane, facing the camera). |
| [<img src="images/fractals/mandelcup.jpg" width="800" alt="Mandelcup">](fractals/mandelcup.md) | **[Mandelcup](fractals/mandelcup.md)** (`mandelcup`)<br>The Mandelcup: each point's direction projected onto a plane, squared there as a complex number and wrapped back onto the sphere, its distance squared. |
| [<img src="images/fractals/quick_dudley.jpg" width="800" alt="Quick Dudley">](fractals/quick_dudley.md) | **[Quick Dudley](fractals/quick_dudley.md)** (`quick_dudley`)<br>Dudley's quadratic: z squared in a three-dimensional algebra whose two imaginary units obey m² = n, n² = m and mn = −1, plus the point, as for the Mandelbrot set. |
| [<img src="images/fractals/collatz.jpg" width="800" alt="Collatz">](fractals/collatz.md) | **[Collatz](fractals/collatz.md)** (`collatz`)<br>Mandelbulber's 3D Collatz fractal: the map (1 + 4z − (1 + 2z) cos πz) / 4, which carries the Collatz rule over to complex numbers, lifted into 3D with cos πz replaced by a half turn of z about (1, 1, 1) whose axis is left unnormalised (so it also stretches along the diagonal). |
| [<img src="images/fractals/collatz_mod.jpg" width="800" alt="Collatz Mod">](fractals/collatz_mod.md) | **[Collatz Mod](fractals/collatz_mod.md)** (`collatz_mod`)<br>Mandelbulber's Collatz Mod: the 3D Collatz map with its turn, axis, offset and scale opened up, and the point optionally added as for a Mandelbrot set. |
| [<img src="images/fractals/newton.jpg" width="800" alt="Newton">](fractals/newton.md) | **[Newton](fractals/newton.md)** (`newton`)<br>A Newton fractal in 3D: Newton's method for z³ = 1 in the triplex (Mandelbulb) algebra, turned inside out so that the basin of the root 1 escapes and the basins of the other two roots make the solid. |
